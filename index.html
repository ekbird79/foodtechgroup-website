Food Labelling & Regulatory Compliance

Food labels must meet specific regulatory requirements under the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code. Errors can result in costly corrective actions, such as applying over-labels to existing stock or reprinting artwork. In more serious cases, non-compliant products may require trade or consumer-level recalls, which can be expensive and damaging to your brand.

Food Tech Group helps businesses develop, review and maintain compliant food labels and technical information, ensuring products meet regulatory requirements and customer expectations.

  • Label development and compliance reviews
  • Ingredient lists and composition checks
  • Nutrition Information Panels
  • Allergen, warning and advisory statements
  • Health and nutrition claims
  • Health Star Rating
  • Compliance checks for imported foods

Food Safety & Quality Management Systems

A robust food safety and quality management system is essential to ensure products are safe, suitable and consistently meet regulatory, customer and business requirements.

Food Tech Group can assist businesses to develop new systems and processes, or review and improve existing programmes to meet New Zealand food safety requirements and align with customer and third-party standards.

  • National Programmes, Food Control Plans and HACCP-based systems
  • GFSI-recognised standards, including BRCGS
  • Supplier and raw material management
  • Allergen management
  • Non-conformance and complaint management
  • Food fraud and food defence
  • Product testing and release requirements
  • Finished product specifications
  • Technical documentation, including Product Information Forms (PIFs)

Internal Auditing & Audit Preparation

Regular internal audits are an important way to verify that your food safety and quality systems are effective, identify opportunities for improvement and ensure your business remains prepared for customer, certification and regulatory audits.

Food Tech Group can assist with internal audits, audit preparation, gap assessments and closing out non-conformances identified during customer, certification or regulatory audits.

  • Identify potential food safety and quality risks
  • Confirm compliance with regulatory and customer requirements
  • Identify opportunities for operational improvement
  • Verify the effectiveness of staff training and awareness
  • Support a positive food safety culture
  • Prepare for customer or third-party audits

Food Import Compliance

Importing food into New Zealand comes with specific regulatory responsibilities. Food importers and distributors must ensure the products they import are safe, suitable and meet New Zealand food safety requirements.

Food Tech Group can assist importers to review product compliance, assess technical documentation and understand their obligations under the Food Act 2014 and the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code.

  • Review of supplier documentation, product specifications and technical information
  • Ingredient, allergen, composition and labelling verification
  • Assessment of applicable New Zealand regulatory requirements and any specific product requirements
  • Identification of compliance gaps and improvement opportunities

Technical Support

Food businesses often face technical challenges that require experienced judgement, practical problem solving and an understanding of the realities of food manufacturing. Food Tech Group provides independent advice on food safety, quality and regulatory matters, helping businesses understand risks and determine practical solutions.

  • Technical problem solving and troubleshooting
  • Investigation of quality issues and customer complaints
  • Review of technical documentation and specifications
  • Regulatory interpretation and guidance
  • Support with corrective actions and improvement plans
  • Assistance responding to customer or supplier technical queries
